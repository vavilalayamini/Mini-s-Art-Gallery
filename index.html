NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked

How do I pay?

At checkout you can choose UPI. The site shows your UPI ID and creates a payment link. After paying, enter the UPI transaction/reference ID before submitting the order.

How is shipping calculated?

Shipping is ₹99 for orders below ₹2,500 and free for orders of ₹2,500 or more. You can change these rules in config.js.

Are these original artworks?

Yes. The current collection is presented as original handmade artwork. Product descriptions and prices can be edited as your catalogue grows.

How do I manage orders?

The included admin dashboard connects to Supabase, where you can see orders, customer details, payment references and update order status. Setup instructions are included.
